# Corrective action plan

Template applied to the worst scorecard supplier after each pipeline run:

| Action | Owner | Due |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Re-promise open POs on demonstrated P90 lead time | Buyer + supplier planner | 10 business days |
| Weekly OTIF huddle with aged late-line list | Supplier performance analyst | Next Monday |
| Qualify alternate source on the top defective material family | Category manager | 45 days |
| Freeze a 4-week forecast fence and share the schedule | Supply planner | 15 days |

Close the loop by watching the same OTIF split (late-only / short-only) the following month — not a blended score alone.
